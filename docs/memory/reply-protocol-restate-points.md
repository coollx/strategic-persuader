In multi-turn discussion sessions, structure every reply point by point: (1) reword the researcher's point as understood, (2) give a one-sentence answer, (3) develop the argument. Never refer to "your point N" without restating it — the researcher brainstorms freely, their views shift, and they will not go back to re-read what they wrote.

**Why:** Explicit instruction on 2026-08-11, after replies that referenced earlier points by number and used compressed abstract sentences ("X supplies the mechanics; Y supplies the language") that transmitted nothing.

**How to apply:** Every substantive reply in a discussion session follows reword → brief answer → development. Spell out concrete objects and actions; if a sentence names no concrete object, rewrite it. Related: [[researcher-rl-fluency]].
