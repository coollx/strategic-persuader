# Backlog

Deferred ideas and directions. One line to one paragraph each: the idea, an optional trigger clause, a provenance date. Future tense only — a made choice is a decision record, not a backlog entry. Entries graduate when a session is spent on them (`/task plan` consumes the entry and deletes it); pruning and deletion are expected. Harness friction is recorded here as `harness:` lines and consumed by the next `/meta` session.
